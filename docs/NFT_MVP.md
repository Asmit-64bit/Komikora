# Comic NFT marketplace MVP

The active NFT pages are `/marketplace`, `/trade` and `/editions/:tokenId`. Each ERC-721 token is one reading edition. Earnings remains accessible when Trade is locked. `/network` reports missing configuration and performs read-only connection checks. `/help` explains the workflow. `/comics/:publicationId`, the old market contract, old deposit accounting and its API remain for existing publications; they are not migrated and their perpetual entitlements do not authorize the NFT reader.

Solidity controls NFT ownership, auctions, bids, escrow, trade eligibility, swaps, fees and credits. The Next server handles wallet sessions, encrypted-content delivery, storage and private inference. Private model execution happens in 0G enclaves, outside the EVM. These service adapters are required for the integrations; there is no separate Express backend.

## Product rules

- Ethereum local development (31337) and Sepolia (11155111), native ETH payments. Drafts support 1–24 pages/panels, with a resized public cover preview from panel 1. One independently timed auction per edition. Supply is fixed at 1–25 per comic version. Updating a minted comic requires a new project/version and new editions.
- Creators sign in with their wallet and accept a rights declaration before submission. Five submissions per wallet account per rolling day. The pilot has no publishing deposit, forfeiture or creator publishing fee: the operator funds Arweave and inference. This requires an operating budget before launch.
- A reviewer-signed EIP-712 permit binds creator, supply, encrypted content hash, public metadata hash, metadata URI hash, license hash, first-auction reserve/duration, deployment, chain and expiry. Supply and license cannot change after minting. The first-auction terms must match the signed terms. The reviewer is an explicit trusted service; its key can authorize minting and delist comics. Keep it secured. The contract has no upgrade or reviewer rotation path.
- Unsold editions remain with the creator. They can enter an auction, be cancelled before any bid, or relisted if no bid met the reserve. Transfers and secondary sales are enabled after the first auction sale. The starting bid is the reserve. Auctions last 1 hour–30 days; new bids increase the highest bid by 5%, at least one wei. Bids in the final two minutes extend the auction, with a hard cap of one hour beyond the original end.
- Settlement can be called by anyone after the deadline. Outbid funds become withdrawable credits. Cancelling an auction with bids is prohibited. Settlement uses internal ERC-721 transfers without receiver callbacks, so a bidder contract cannot trap everyone else's funds by rejecting an NFT callback. Contract-wallet bidders must support managing ERC-721 tokens.
- Primary ETH sales pay 2.5% to the treasury and the remainder to the creator. Secondary ETH sales pay 2.5% to the treasury, 5% to the creator and 92.5% to the seller. ERC-2981 communicates the royalty to other marketplaces; enforcement elsewhere is not guaranteed. Collaborator shares in the studio remain an offchain agreement; the original creator receives primary proceeds and royalties.
- Secondary fixed-price asks expire and automatically clear on every transfer, including escrow deposits. A buy checks the expected price. Direct transfers do not change sale history. Secondary auctions are also supported.
- Every first sale is offered in Marketplace. Trade unlocks only while the connected wallet beneficially holds at least one edition with a completed first sale. Unsold creator inventory does not qualify. Sold comics in auction/swap escrow still count for their depositor. Giving away or selling the last comic relocks Trade. The contract also rejects secondary purchases and secondary auction bids from empty collections. Cancellations, reclamation, settlement and credit withdrawals remain available; Earnings can always be reached.
- Swaps use the last completed auction price. Equal-value comics exchange directly; lower-value comics require the exact ETH difference. A higher-value offer is rejected. Both comics and the top-up are escrowed. Cancellation, expiry and unselected-offer reclamation return assets to their depositors. Swaps do not change first/latest sale references because barter is not an unambiguous ETH sale. No platform fee or royalty is collected on swaps.
- The contract preserves first sale price/time, latest ETH sale price/time, and last auction price separately. All on-platform sale and bid events are public; off-platform sales and bundles are not treated as verified prices.

## Reading and storage

Full pages and captions are AES-256-GCM encrypted before Arweave upload. Only public cover art, license, metadata and explained AI findings are public. Keys are wrapped with `CONTENT_KEY_ENCRYPTION_KEY` and retained by the application. Back up `.data`, the encryption key, and deployment configuration securely; losing them can make paid comics unreadable. The pilot file store supports a single application process, not multiple independent writers.

`/api/editions/read/:tokenId` requires an authenticated wallet session and checks ownership at a confirmed block, then rechecks after fetching and decrypting. The author has no special bypass. A sold token in auction/swap escrow remains readable by its depositor until settlement. Delisting prevents new bids/listings/buys; existing bids may still settle. Delisting does not revoke existing ownership or reading access.

The reader checks every 15 seconds and clears pages after 20 seconds without a successful check. The chain confirmation delay is additional: `CHAIN_CONFIRMATIONS` defaults to zero locally and two blocks on Sepolia. Responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`; clear text is never written to Arkiv. Previously delivered pages cannot be recalled or prevented from being copied. Private hosting availability remains a dependency. Wallet recovery is handled by the wallet provider; there is no admin token recovery.

## Review and moderation

The NFT 0G adapter submits actual images. File validity is checked deterministically; the model assesses completeness, legibility and order with advisory findings. Model, prompt version, manifest hash, timestamp, request ID, reasons and provider verification status are recorded. Provider attestation does not establish copyright or score correctness. A failed service call produces no mint permit and no creator penalty; uploaded data is reused on retry.

Private Computer uses `ZG_COMPUTE_API_KEY`, `ZG_COMPUTE_BASE_URL` and the image-capable `0gm-1.0-35b-a3b` model by default. `ZG_COMPUTE_TRUST_MODE` must be `private`: there is no standard-routing fallback. Results require the sealed-response marker, a provider address, and positive gateway-reported DCAP, on-chain signer and OS measurement checks. Those checks are labeled **gateway-reported**, not independently verified by this application. The gateway verifies provider response signatures; independent gateway verification additionally requires the official `pcverify -gateway router-api.0g.ai -strict` procedure. A live generated-comic image test passed on this private route. See [0G's gateway verification procedure](https://github.com/0gfoundation/0g-pc-e2ee/blob/main/docs/verifying-the-gateway.md). Legacy direct-broker review uses separate `ZG_PRIVATE_KEY` settings and local provider signature checks.

Marketplace includes optional collector suggestions. Signed-in collectors can enter interests; private inference recommends only eligible active first-sale auctions, returns links and a request ID, and rejects invented or duplicate IDs. Requests are limited to 15 per day per account and cached for an hour. Interests remain in private application storage and are not sent to Arkiv. These are reading suggestions, not investment advice.

## Wallet publishing and moderation

Wallet signatures authenticate every account. Creators submit a rights declaration and their saved comic for review, then confirm the mint transaction in their wallet. There is no external identity redirect or separate identity approval. A wallet account does not establish authorship or one-human-one-account uniqueness; daily submission limits apply per wallet account.

After review succeeds, the authenticated project owner can request a reviewer-signed mint permit. The permit binds the saved content, supply, license, auction terms, deployment and creator wallet, and expires ten minutes after the later of the current chain block timestamp and server clock. The server records its expiry before signing. The NFT contract requires the author wallet to submit the mint and prevents reuse of a comic key. Minting is the creator's final confirmation of the terms.

The retained legacy market uses its original `PersonhoodCertificate` ABI and `purchaseRequiresPersonhood` policy flag for compatibility with existing deployments. Those names now refer to wallet-session authorization only and provide no personhood assurance. `/api/market/authorization` issues purchase certificates to authenticated wallet sessions. Publication quotes include their matching legacy authorization automatically. New legacy deployments disable the optional purchase-certificate policy by default. NFT editions do not use these certificates and require no contract change for this removal.

Flagged/borderline results go to a human appeal. The target response deadline is 72 hours; the pilot requires a staffed reviewer and does not auto-approve missed deadlines. The author may reopen unminted work for corrections. Outstanding mint permits must expire first; their deadline uses the later of the chain block timestamp and server clock plus ten minutes. Already-minted works cannot be reopened. Decisions include a public reason while appeal text stays private to the author and reviewer.

Signed-in users can report infringement/abuse, limited to ten reports per day. The NFT moderation panel lets the designated reviewer resolve reports and delist on chain. This is a reporting and sale-stoppage mechanism, not a full legal takedown/remedy system. Public Arweave data cannot be erased. Illegal-content handling, access restrictions required by law, refunds for unavailable content, reviewer staffing and operating budgets need an explicit launch policy. There is no automatic clawback of settled NFT sales.

## Local setup

1. `npm install` and `npm run env:setup`. Preserve existing secrets and data.
2. Start `npm run contracts:node` in a separate terminal.
3. Set `EDITION_REVIEWER_ADDRESS` and `EDITION_TREASURY_ADDRESS` in `contracts/.env`. For local-only deployments choose development accounts. Run `npm run editions:deploy:local`.
4. Copy the printed address to `NEXT_PUBLIC_EDITIONS_ADDRESS` in `.env.local`. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_CHAIN_ID=31337`, `CHAIN_RPC_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8545` and `EDITION_REVIEWER_PRIVATE_KEY` matching the deployed reviewer. Never put a private key in a `NEXT_PUBLIC_` variable.
5. Configure Arweave, content encryption and the funded 0G Private Computer account using the environment template. Run `npm run dev` or rebuild after changing public configuration. The NFT flow does not require a legacy market deployment.
6. Sepolia deployment uses `npm run editions:deploy:sepolia`, the existing Sepolia RPC and deployer settings. `DEPLOY_PREFLIGHT=1` estimates gas and checks balance without broadcasting. Actual deployment saves its transaction, block, constructor arguments and runtime code hash under `contracts/deployments/`. Existing deployment manifests require explicit overwrite configuration. No public-chain deployment has been performed: the funded deployment account and deployment settings remain missing.

## Arkiv

Set `INDEXER_EDITIONS_ADDRESS`, `INDEXER_SOURCE_RPC_URL`, `EDITIONS_START_BLOCK`, `INDEXER_CONFIRMATIONS`, `ARKIV_ACCESS_KEY` and a funded `ARKIV_PRIVATE_KEY` in `indexer/.env`. Run `npm run editions:index -- --dry-run` to inspect public projections, then `npm run editions:index -- --watch` to write. This is a separate index from legacy publication records.

The NFT index rebuilds from confirmed events and contract state, writes stable records for editions and public events (including bids, sales, transfers and swaps), reconciles records removed by reorganizations, and renews 180-day entities when fewer than 30 days remain. Watch mode retries a failed reconciliation after 30 seconds. Run it under a process supervisor; it is an MVP full reconciliation, not an incremental large-scale indexer. Snapshot data contains first/latest price, currency, dates, auction price and asking price. It includes no proofs, content keys, private pages or private appeal text.

Configure the trusted `ARKIV_WRITER_ADDRESS` in the application for indexed primary/secondary discovery. `ARKIV_ACCESS_KEY` is optional on the public discovery RPC and may be needed for reliable rate limits. Price, ownership, access, and transaction validity are always checked against the contract. When discovery is unavailable or the index is empty the UI falls back to paginated RPC reads; text filters apply within the current page. Index lag can temporarily omit a recent edition; refresh or use My editions.

## Validation and launch work

- `npm run contracts:test`: contract tests including auction limits, escrow accounting, signature tampering, resale royalties, stale asks, transfers, swaps and reading ownership.
- `npm test`: encryption, wallet authorization, review, submission and ownership-gated reader unit tests.
- `npm run build && npm run test:editions`: isolated real-chain/SIWE/HTTP reader integration. Synthetic encrypted storage and approved-content fixtures are explicit; this does not claim live 0G screening.
- `npm run check`, `npm run test:e2e`: lint, type checks and browser regression tests.
- `LIVE_0G=1 node --env-file=.env.local node_modules/vitest/vitest.mjs run tests/integration/private-computer.live.test.ts`: opt-in actual private image review using generated comic art; consumes account inference credit. The ordinary test suite skips live service calls.

Before a pilot, test wallet onboarding and run real comic images through the chosen 0G vision provider; measure costs, false flags, publication completion, transfers, purchases, resales and reader failures. Validate Arweave durability/funding and live Arkiv rebuild/renewal. Supply credentials, choose and fund the deployment, staff moderation, define buyer remedies, and review/audit the contracts before real-value use. These operational tasks cannot be established by local tests.
